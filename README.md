# ♥ Happy Boyfriend's Day - Little Corner Of The Internet ♥

A custom, interactive romantic website created with love for your boyfriend, inspired by the Lovehearts template.

---

## 🌟 What's Included

1. **Interactive Opening Envelope (`#s1`)**:
   - Vintage pastel envelope with wax heart seal & cat sticker
   - Tap anywhere to open with romantic heart confetti & music playback

2. **Hero & Music Player (`#s2`)**:
   - Polaroid photo with washi tape styling
   - Sweet message card & animated love stickers
   - **Music Player**:
     - Pre-configured for **"Rathinamo (Female Version)" by Tanisha Gnanavel & Saurav Srisan**
     - Live progress bar, timestamp, and play/pause controls
     - Mini equalizer pill button at the top-right
     - Option to select any MP3 directly from your device

3. **The Memory Wall (`#s3`)**:
   - 6 Interactive 3D Polaroid flip cards with golden push pins
   - Click/tap any card to flip it over and read the sweet handwritten message on the back!

4. **Officially Official Boyfriend Certificate (`#s4`)**:
   - Vintage framed certificate with ornate gold border and official seal
   - Framed portrait of him with romantic duties (Chief Banter Officer, 24/7 Hotline, Fries Policy, etc.)
   - **Download Certificate Button**: Exports a crystal-clear high-res PNG (`Boyfriend-Certificate.png`) ready for Instagram or printing!

5. **A Love Note For You (`#s5`)**:
   - Authentic lined notebook paper with handwritten cursive font
   - Surrounded by pinned polaroid moments of you two
   - "Send Love & Confetti" celebration button with continuous heart bursts
   - Replay button

6. **Quick Personalize Button (`✏️ Customize`)**:
   - Click the button at the top-right anytime to easily tweak your boyfriend's name, your name, or your personal letter message!

## 🎵 How To Add Your Song (`Rathinamo - Female Version`)

You can set up the music in two easy ways:

### Method 1: Drop the MP3 in the folder (Recommended)
1. Download your MP3 audio of *Rathinamo (Female Version)*.
2. Rename the file to `song.mp3`.
3. Place it directly inside this folder (`c:\Users\JEEVITHA\Desktop\workora4\US\song.mp3`).
4. That's it! When you open the website, it will play automatically when the envelope is tapped.

### Method 2: Select it in the browser
1. Open the website in your browser.
2. In the music player on Page 2, click **"📁 Choose another MP3 file / song"**.
3. Select your audio file and it will play immediately!

*(Note: Even before adding your file, a soft ambient romantic chime will play so there is never silence!)*

## 🚀 How To Open & View Locally

Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox!

Or to preview via a local server:
```bash
# In this directory:
python -m http.server 3000
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:3000`
